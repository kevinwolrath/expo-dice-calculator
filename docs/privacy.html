Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Dice Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Dice Calculator mobile application.

Information We Collect

Dice Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

The application does not require you to create an account or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

Data Stored on Your Device

Information created while using Dice Calculator, such as dice calculations, settings, or other application data, may be stored locally on your device.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us unless a feature within the application explicitly states otherwise.

Removing the application may also remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and operating system.

Internet and Network Access

Some versions or features of Dice Calculator may use an internet connection to access application services or retrieve application data.

Where network access is used, Dice Calculator does not use that connection to sell your personal information or track you for advertising purposes.

Analytics and Advertising

Dice Calculator does not currently use third-party advertising services or analytics services to track users.

Third-Party Services

The application may rely on services provided by the device operating system or application distribution platform, such as Google Play or Apple App Store.

Those services may independently collect technical or usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Their collection and processing of information is outside the control of Dice Calculator.

Children's Privacy

Dice Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the application does not require users to provide personal information, no age-specific personal information is intentionally collected.

Data Security

Where application data is stored locally, access to that data is generally protected by the security features of your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Dice Calculator changes, particularly if future versions introduce accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or other services that process user information.

Any updated policy will include a revised "Last updated" date.
